Support

How can we help?

Having trouble with iScreenMate? Whether you have an activation issue, found a bug, or simply have a question, we're here to help.

Contact support

Send us an email and describe what you're experiencing. Including your macOS version and iScreenMate version will help us troubleshoot the issue faster.

Email Support

Before contacting support

  1. Check your iScreenMate version.
    You can find the application version in the About section of iScreenMate.
  2. Check your macOS version.
    Open Apple menu → About This Mac to see which version of macOS you're using.
  3. Describe what happened.
    Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  4. Include an error message if available.
    A screenshot or exact error message can help us identify the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find my license key?

Your license information is provided after purchasing iScreenMate through the authorized purchase platform. Check your purchase confirmation email for details.

Can I use iScreenMate on another Mac?

Personal licenses are associated with the individual user rather than a specific Mac. If you replace your Mac, you can generally activate iScreenMate on your new device.

See our Software License Agreement for details.

Do my screenshots leave my Mac?

No. Screenshots captured by iScreenMate are designed to remain on your Mac. They are not uploaded to our servers.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Does iScreenMate require an internet connection?

Screenshot capture itself is designed to work locally. An internet connection may be required for certain features such as license activation, license verification, or checking for updates.

Why does iScreenMate request Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires applications that capture screen content to have the appropriate Screen Recording permission. iScreenMate uses this permission to capture the selected area of your screen.

What information should I include in a bug report?

Please include your iScreenMate version, macOS version, Mac architecture if known, and a description of the problem.

If iScreenMate provides a crash or error report, you may include it with your message.

Can I request a refund?

Yes. Refund requests are reviewed according to our Refund Policy.

Reporting a bug

We appreciate detailed bug reports. They help us reproduce problems and make iScreenMate better.

A useful bug report includes:
  • iScreenMate version
  • macOS version
  • Mac model or architecture, if known
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Any error message or crash information

Privacy and support requests

Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, private documents, or other sensitive information in support emails.

If you voluntarily send us a screenshot or diagnostic information to help investigate a problem, it will be used for the purpose of handling your support request.

For more information about how information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.
