Refund Policy

Last updated — August 24, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests for iScreenMate purchases are handled.

1. General Information

iScreenMate is digital software. When you purchase a license, you receive access to a digital product rather than a physical item.

Because digital products can be downloaded and used immediately, refund eligibility may depend on the applicable purchase terms, the payment provider, and mandatory consumer protection laws.

2. Where You Purchased iScreenMate

If you purchased iScreenMate through Gumroad, payment processing and the purchase transaction may be handled by Gumroad.

In that case, refund requests may be subject to the applicable Gumroad policies and the terms presented to you at the time of purchase.

Where appropriate, you may also contact iScreenMate support regarding your purchase.

3. Refund Requests

If you would like to request a refund, please contact support using the email address provided below.

Please include:
  • The email address used for the purchase.
  • Your order or transaction information, if available.
  • A brief explanation of the reason for the refund request.

Providing sufficient purchase information helps us identify the transaction and process your request more efficiently.

4. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same product more than once, please contact support with the relevant transaction information.

Duplicate transactions may be reviewed for a refund where appropriate.

5. Technical Problems

If iScreenMate does not work as expected, we encourage you to contact support before requesting a refund.

We may be able to help resolve installation, activation, compatibility, or other technical issues.

When contacting support, it can be helpful to provide:
  • iScreenMate version.
  • macOS version.
  • Mac hardware or processor type.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Relevant error messages or crash information.

6. License Activation Problems

If you purchased iScreenMate but cannot activate your license, please contact support before requesting a refund.

We may be able to assist with activation or determine whether the license was correctly issued.

7. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits or excludes any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you under the laws of your country or region.

If applicable law provides you with a statutory right to cancel a digital purchase or obtain a refund, that right remains unaffected by this policy.

8. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, the refund will generally be processed through the payment method or payment provider used for the original transaction.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

9. Effect of a Refund

If a purchase is refunded, the associated software license may be deactivated or revoked.

Continued use of a refunded license may not be permitted.

10. Changes to This Refund Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the product, payment process, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the applicable revision date.
