Privacy Policy

Last updated — August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iScreenMate collects, uses, and protects information when you use the iScreenMate application and this website.

1. Who We Are

iScreenMate is a macOS screenshot application developed and distributed under the brand DSGN & MARKETING.

Developer / Data Controller Volodymyr O.
DSGN & MARKETING
Email: supportiscreenmate@gmail.com

2. Privacy by Design

iScreenMate is designed to keep your screenshots on your Mac.

The application performs screenshot capture, editing, copying, and saving locally on your device whenever possible.

What iScreenMate does not do
  • It does not upload screenshots to our servers.
  • It does not store screenshots in the cloud.
  • It does not create a user account.
  • It does not require you to sign in.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled in the application, iScreenMate may collect limited technical information necessary to provide updates, licensing, and diagnostic support.

Technical Information

  • Installed iScreenMate version.
  • macOS version.
  • Mac hardware architecture (Intel or Apple Silicon).
  • Language or region settings.
  • Application configuration relevant to diagnostics.

This information is used only for compatibility, troubleshooting, and update delivery.

Crash & Diagnostic Information (Optional)

If crash reporting is enabled in a future version of iScreenMate, the application may send anonymous diagnostic information after a crash.

Crash reports may include
  • Error or crash code.
  • Stack trace.
  • Application version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device architecture.
  • Time of the crash.

Crash reports are used solely to identify bugs and improve application stability.

4. Screenshots and Screen Content

Screenshots captured using iScreenMate remain stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them using another application or service.

We do not collect or upload
  • Screenshot images.
  • Clipboard image contents.
  • Screen recordings.
  • Files saved to your Mac.
  • Contents of your desktop, documents, or applications.

5. Clipboard Access

When you choose "Copy to Clipboard", iScreenMate places the captured image into the macOS clipboard locally on your device.

Clipboard contents are not transmitted to DSGN & MARKETING.

6. macOS Permissions

iScreenMate may request macOS permissions required for screenshot functionality.

  • Screen Recording permission (required by macOS to capture screen content).
  • Accessibility permission (only if a future feature requires it).

These permissions are managed entirely through macOS System Settings and may be revoked at any time.

7. Software Updates

iScreenMate may check whether a newer version of the application is available.

Update checks may include:

  • Current application version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device architecture.

Update checks do not include screenshots or personal files.

8. Purchases and License Keys

Purchases of iScreenMate are processed through Gumroad.

Gumroad may process information necessary to complete your purchase, including payment details and your email address.

If license key activation is enabled, iScreenMate may communicate with Gumroad's licensing service to verify the validity of a license key.

License verification may include
  • License key.
  • Application version.
  • Activation request status.

Payment information is processed by Gumroad according to Gumroad's own Privacy Policy.

9. Third-Party Services

iScreenMate may rely on trusted third-party services to provide certain functionality.

  • Gumroad — payments and license management.
  • Software update infrastructure.
  • Crash reporting provider (if enabled in future releases).

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy.

10. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to:

  • Deliver software updates.
  • Verify software licenses.
  • Fix crashes and software bugs.
  • Improve compatibility with macOS versions.
  • Respond to support requests.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

11. Data Retention

Diagnostic information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate technical issues and improve the application.

Support emails may be retained while resolving your request.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information that we receive.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access.
  • Request correction.
  • Request deletion.
  • Object to certain processing where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the email below.

14. Children's Privacy

iScreenMate is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. International Users

If you access iScreenMate from outside your country of residence, your information may be processed in accordance with applicable laws governing the services we use, including payment providers.
