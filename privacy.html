Privacy Policy

Last updated — August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iScreenMate collects, uses, and protects information when you use the iScreenMate application and related website.

1. Who We Are

iScreenMate is developed and distributed under the brand DSGN & MARKETING.

This Privacy Policy applies to the iScreenMate macOS application, its website, and related support interactions.

2. Privacy by Design

iScreenMate is designed with a local-first approach. Screenshot capture, editing, copying, and saving are performed locally on your Mac.

iScreenMate does not:
  • Upload your screenshots to our servers.
  • Store screenshots in the cloud.
  • Require you to create a user account.
  • Require you to sign in to use the application.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available in a particular version of iScreenMate, limited technical information may be processed for licensing, software updates, diagnostics, and support.

Technical Information

This may include:

  • iScreenMate version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device architecture, such as Intel or Apple Silicon.
  • Language or regional settings where relevant.
  • Technical information required to diagnose compatibility issues.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

If crash reporting or diagnostic functionality is enabled in a particular version of iScreenMate, the application may transmit limited technical information following an error or crash.

Diagnostic information may include:
  • Error or crash information.
  • Application version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device architecture.
  • Time of the event.
  • Technical information contained in a crash report.

Diagnostic information is used to investigate problems, improve stability, and improve compatibility.

4. Screenshots and Screen Content

Screenshots captured using iScreenMate are processed and stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them using another application or service.

We do not collect or upload:
  • Screenshot images.
  • Clipboard image contents.
  • Screen recordings.
  • Files saved to your Mac.
  • The contents of your desktop or other applications.

5. Clipboard Access

When you choose to copy a screenshot to the clipboard, iScreenMate places the captured image into the macOS clipboard locally on your device.

Clipboard contents are not transmitted to us by iScreenMate.

6. macOS Permissions

iScreenMate may request macOS permissions required for its screenshot functionality.

  • Screen Recording permission, which macOS requires for capturing screen content.
  • Accessibility permission if required by a particular feature or future version.

These permissions are controlled by macOS and can be reviewed or revoked through System Settings.

7. Software Updates

iScreenMate may check whether a newer version of the application is available.

An update check may process limited technical information such as:

  • Current application version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device architecture.

Update checks do not include screenshots or personal files.

8. Purchases and License Keys

iScreenMate purchases may be processed through Gumroad or another authorized payment provider.

The payment provider may process information necessary to complete your purchase, such as your email address and payment information.

If license activation is supported, iScreenMate may communicate with the relevant licensing service to verify the validity of a license key.

License verification may involve:
  • License key or license identifier.
  • Application version.
  • Activation or verification status.

Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy policy.

9. Third-Party Services

iScreenMate may use third-party services to provide certain functionality.

  • Gumroad or another payment provider for purchases.
  • A licensing service for license verification, if applicable.
  • Software update infrastructure.
  • A crash reporting or diagnostic service, if enabled.

Third-party services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. How Information Is Used

Information that we receive may be used to:

  • Deliver software updates.
  • Verify software licenses.
  • Investigate crashes and technical problems.
  • Improve application stability and compatibility.
  • Respond to support requests.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

11. Support Communications

If you contact support by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address, message, attachments, and technical information you voluntarily include.

Support information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot reported problems.

12. Data Retention

Technical and diagnostic information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, product improvement, security, or other legitimate business purposes.

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support and maintain relevant records.

13. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information that we receive.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the email address below.

15. Children's Privacy

iScreenMate is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

16. International Users

Depending on the services used by iScreenMate, information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required, applicable safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as iScreenMate evolves or as legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page and will include an updated revision date.
